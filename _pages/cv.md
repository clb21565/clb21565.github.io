## Contributions to Externally Funded Research

**Community Prevalence and Transmission Pathways of Multidrug Resistant Carbapenemase-producing Enterobacteriaceae** · *2024*
NIH R01 (2024–2029 est.) · PI: Latania Logan (Emory University); co-PI: Amy Pruden
*Contribution:* Bioinformatics expertise for source-tracking methodology, selection of culture methods, and multiple rounds of editing.

**Quantifying Wastewater Sources of Antibiotic Resistance to Aquatic and Soil Environments and Associated Human Health Risks** ($2,374,575) · *2024*
EPA (2024–2027 est.) · PI: Lola Olabode (Water Research Foundation)
*Contribution:* Bioinformatics expertise; proposed novel computational methods for predicting multivariate antibiotic susceptibility profiles of pathogens from metagenomic data alone.

**Advancing Comprehensive Wastewater Surveillance of Endemic and Emerging Pathogens Across Multiple Microbial Domains through Strain-resolved Metagenomics** ($584,968) · *2022*
CDC (2022–2024) · PI: Amy Pruden (VT); Co-PIs: Liqing Zhang (VT), Peter Vikesland (VT)
*Contribution:* Lead author for the Pruden lab: proposal development, project management, and lab methods and software for a protocol recommended to the CDC National Wastewater Surveillance System (NWSS) capturing multiple pathogens and ARGs.

**WRF 4961: The Use of NGS Technologies and Metagenomics Approaches to Evaluate Water and Wastewater Quality Monitoring and Treatment Technologies** ($490,015) · *2020*
Water Research Foundation (2020–2023) · PI: Amy Pruden (VT); Co-PI: Emily Garner (WVU)
*Contribution:* Co-wrote a guidance document for US water utilities on NGS; primary author of the chapter on metagenomic assembly algorithms.

**Frameworks: Developing Cyberinfrastructure for Waterborne Antibiotic Resistance Risk Surveillance (CI4-WARS)** ($1,299,036) · *2020*
NSF (2020–2024) · PI: Liqing Zhang (VT); Co-PIs: Amy Pruden, Peter Vikesland, Ali Butt (VT)
*Contribution:* Led sampling for a prototype metagenomics-based monitoring program at three full-scale WWTPs: >1,000 samples and 11 × 10¹² bp of Illumina and nanopore sequence.
