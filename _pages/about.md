I'm a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Chemical and Biosciences at Aalborg University (with Mads Albertsen). I work at the intersection of bioinformatics, environmental microbiology, and public health. My focus is how antibiotic resistance genes emerge, move between bacteria on mobile genetic elements, and spread through wastewater systems.

My research combines short- and long-read metagenomics, strain- and microdiversity-resolved analyses, and curated reference resources such as [mobileOG-db](https://mobileogdb.flsi.cloud.vt.edu). I use them to:

- relate antibiotic resistance in sewage to community antibiotic use and clinical resistance;
- quantify selection and horizontal gene transfer during wastewater treatment;
- recover and characterize environmental mobile genetic elements;
- develop predictive wastewater-based antibiotic resistance indicators for public health. 

I completed my Ph.D. in Genetics, Bioinformatics, and Computational Biology at Virginia Tech with Amy Pruden.
